## 3. SEO Quality Standards for Every Article
1. **Clear Search Intent:** Direct answer in the first 150 words.
2. **Authoritativeness:** Practical tips derived from real live sound production and artist booking scenarios.
3. **Internal Links:** Minimum 3 contextual links to relevant service pages (`/wedding-singer-booking`, `/pricing`, `/artists`).
4. **Structured Data:** Valid `Article` or `BlogPosting` schema with headline, dates, author organization, and publisher logo.
