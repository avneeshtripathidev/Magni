## 3. Keyword Cannibalization Prevention Matrix

| Potential Cannibalization Conflict | Page A (Primary Target) | Page B (Secondary Target) | Resolution Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"live singers in delhi" vs "singer in delhi"** | `/live-singers-in-delhi` (Broad directory) | `/singer-in-delhi` | Canonicalize `/singer-in-delhi` as authoritative, cross-link cleanly. |
| **"delhi" vs "singer in delhi"** | `/city/delhi` (Regional Hub) | `/singer-in-delhi` (Service specific) | `/city/delhi` serves as city hub; links out to `/singer-in-delhi`, `/dj-in-delhi`, `/live-band-in-delhi`. |
| **"book singer" vs "hire singer"** | `/artists` (Directory) | `/how-to-book` (Process guide) | `/artists` captures booking intent; `/how-to-book` answers procedural and escrow/guarantee questions. |
| **"singer booking price" vs "/pricing"** | `/pricing` (Full pricing table) | `/singer-booking-price` | Distinct pages: `/pricing` covers tiered packages; `/singer-booking-price` provides localized price ranges with links to `/pricing`. |

## 4. Implementation Rules for Content & Engineering
1. **No Keyword Stuffing:** Anchor text in internal links must use natural variations (`verified live singers in Delhi`, `hire acoustic guitarists`, `wedding entertainment packages`).
2. **Controlled Slugs:** Slugs follow lowercase kebab-case (`/live-singer-in-delhi`). Non-canonical variants redirect with HTTP 301.
3. **Intent Differentiation:** Informational queries are strictly routed to the `/blog-post/` architecture; transactional queries are routed to `[location_slug]` or `/artists`.
