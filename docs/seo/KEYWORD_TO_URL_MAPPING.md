## Internal Anchor Text Guidelines
1. **Contextual Diversity:** Anchor text must fit the natural flow of content (e.g., "...if you are organizing an intimate gathering, you can [book a live singer for your house party](/singer-for-house-party) with full sound equipment...").
2. **Avoid Raw URLs:** Never use naked URLs as visible anchor text in editorial content.
3. **Pillar Hubs:** All localized sub-pages (e.g., `/singer-for-house-party-in-delhi`) link back up to both the regional city hub (`/city/delhi`) and the primary category hub (`/artists` or `/singer-for-house-party`).
